# How to actually learn Chinese with your lab

The app is finished enough. The tool is not the problem now — the habit is.
This is a simple daily plan. Small and daily beats big and rare.

## The one rule

**15 minutes every day beats 2 hours once a week.** Your memory grows from
seeing a word many times on different days, not from one long session.

## Daily (about 15–20 minutes)

1. **Study Mode — review due cards (10 min).**
   Open Study Mode, level = All, and clear your "Due" cards. Rate honestly:
   if you didn't remember it fast, it is not "Easy". Honest rating is what
   makes spaced repetition work. Hit your daily goal (start at 15 cards).

2. **One practice mode, rotating (5 min).**
   - Mon / Thu → Sentences (words in context)
   - Tue / Fri → Listen (train your ears)
   - Wed / Sat → Tones (be understood)
   - Sunday → rest, or redo your weakest one.
   When you miss a word here, it comes back in Study Mode automatically.

3. **Say 3 sentences out loud (2 min).**  ← the part you skip
   In Sentences or Listen, read the example sentence aloud, copying the audio.
   The app builds input. Only your mouth builds output. No app can do this
   for you. Do it even if it feels silly.

## Weekly (about 20 minutes, once)

- **One Grammar card, deeply.** Read one pattern (了, 把, 被, 是…的, 比…).
  Then write 2 of your OWN sentences using it. This is how you move from
  knowing words to making sentences.
- **Three hard characters.** Open the character detail for 3 characters you
  keep failing. Look at the radical, the shape, and the other words that use
  them. Seeing the web helps them stick.

## How to keep it efficient

- **Rate honestly.** Marking "Easy" to feel good just makes the word vanish
  and come back when you've forgotten it. Waste of time.
- **Don't add lessons faster than you study them.** New words feel like
  progress but they are not learning until reviewed. Study first, add later.
- **Watch two numbers only:** your streak, and "words learned". If both go up
  each week, you are winning. Ignore everything else.
- **Speak.** Read aloud daily, and if you can, talk to a real person once a
  week. This is the single biggest thing missing from your practice.
